Keepworthy — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 14, 2026
1. Overview
Keepworthy is a private, personal-use macOS application designed to help its owner organize, research, and manage a collection of trading cards and other collectibles.
Keepworthy is not commercially distributed and is not available to external users.
2. Information Accessed
With the account owner's authorization, Keepworthy may access information through eBay's APIs, including:
- Public marketplace listings, prices, and product information
- The owner's eBay purchase history and transaction details
- The owner's selling activity, listings, and related financial information
Keepworthy accesses only information necessary for its collection-management and research functions.
3. Data Storage and Use
Keepworthy is designed to store collection records and related information locally on the owner's Mac.
Information obtained through eBay may be used to support collection management, purchase tracking, marketplace research, and resale analysis.
Keepworthy does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.
4. Data Security
Keepworthy uses macOS security features to protect locally stored information.
Where applicable, authentication credentials and authorization tokens are stored securely using macOS Keychain.
Access to eBay account information requires authorization through eBay's authentication services.
5. Data Retention and Deletion
Collection information is retained locally until the owner chooses to modify or delete it.
The owner controls the application's locally stored collection records.
eBay-derived information is retained only as permitted by applicable eBay API requirements.
6. Third-Party Services
Keepworthy may communicate with eBay and other research providers to retrieve marketplace information.
Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.
eBay's privacy policy is available at:
https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy?id=4260
7. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated as Keepworthy's functionality and integrations evolve.
The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.
8. Contact
Keepworthy is developed and maintained privately for personal use.
Questions regarding this policy may be directed to the application owner.
